How to cook

How to choose

A vivid body colour with clear orange spots. Clear eyes and a firm body.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The firm flesh holds for two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Fukuoka
  • Kyoto
  • Niigata

Good to know

Called ako in Kansai, where it is prized as a summer luxury. The orange spots covering the body resemble pheasant feathers, which is where the Japanese name comes from. Stocks are now released in several areas to protect the species.
