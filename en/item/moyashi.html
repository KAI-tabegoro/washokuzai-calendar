How to cook

How to choose

Thick white stems with roots not browned. They should look translucent and unbroken.

Storage

They spoil quickly. Refrigerate in the bag and use the same or the next day. Standing them in water extends this.

Main areas

  • Tochigi
  • Fukushima
  • Gunma
  • Niigata

Good to know

Mung bean sprouts are the most widely sold; soybean sprouts go into Korean cooking, and black gram sprouts are preferred in Kansai. Germinated in darkened factories, they are unaffected by weather and are one of the few vegetables whose price stays steady all year.
