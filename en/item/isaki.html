How to choose

A yellowish body, good thickness and clear eyes.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. Use within one or two days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Shimane
  • Chiba
  • Ehime

Good to know

Fattest during the rainy season, when it is called tsuyu-isaki. Young fish have yellow stripes and are nicknamed uribo.
