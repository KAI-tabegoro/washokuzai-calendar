How to cook

How to choose

Bright green leaves with a strong aroma, free of bruises and dark spots.

Storage

Stand the stems in water and refrigerate, or wrap in damp paper and seal.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Shizuoka
  • Oita
  • Ibaraki

Good to know

Green shiso is used as a garnish, red shiso for pickled plums and cordials. They are varieties of the same plant.
