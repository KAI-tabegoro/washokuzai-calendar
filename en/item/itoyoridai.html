How to cook

How to choose

Vivid pink with clear yellow stripes. Clear eyes and a firm body.

Storage

The flesh is soft and watery. Gut and refrigerate, using within two days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Shimane
  • Fukuoka
  • Kochi

Good to know

The upper lobe of the tail fin extends into a long thread, which gives the fish its Japanese name. Though called a bream, it belongs to the threadfin bream family rather than the sea bream family. Pale pink with yellow stripes, it is valued in Japanese cooking for its looks as much as its taste.
