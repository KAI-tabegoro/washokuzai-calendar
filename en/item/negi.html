How to cook

How to choose

A long, firm white section with a clear boundary to the green, tightly wrapped.

Storage

Wrap in newspaper and store upright somewhere cool and dark. Sliced leek should be sealed and refrigerated or frozen.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Saitama
  • Ibaraki
  • Gunma

Good to know

Kanto eats mainly the white part, Kansai mainly the green.
