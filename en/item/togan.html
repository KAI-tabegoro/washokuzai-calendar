How to cook

How to choose

A white bloom on the skin, heavy in the hand, with no damage.

Storage

Whole, it holds for months somewhere cool and dark. Once cut, remove the pith, wrap and refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Okinawa
  • Aichi
  • Okayama
  • Kanagawa

Good to know

Harvested in summer, but named for keeping whole until winter. More than ninety per cent water with almost no flavour of its own, it is cooked to take up the taste of dashi. Held to cool the body, it has long been eaten in hot weather.
