How to cook

How to choose

Thick, deep green leaves on sturdy stems. Yellow sap at the cut end means it is fresh.

Storage

Wrap in damp paper and refrigerate standing upright. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Tokyo
  • Chiba
  • Shizuoka
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

The name comes from its vigour: pick it today and a new shoot appears tomorrow. It grows wild on the coasts of the Izu Islands and the Boso Peninsula, and is known as a speciality of Hachijojima. The yellow sap from a cut stem contains polyphenols.
