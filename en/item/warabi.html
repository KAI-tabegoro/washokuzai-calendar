How to cook

How to choose

Tips still tightly curled, on thick straight stems with a fresh cut end.

Storage

It must be de-bittered. Steep overnight in hot water with baking soda or wood ash, then keep in water in the fridge.

Main areas

  • Akita
  • Yamagata
  • Fukushima
  • Niigata

Good to know

It is never eaten without first drawing out the bitterness: steeped overnight in hot water with baking soda or wood ash, then the water changed in the morning. Starch from the rhizome is true warabi flour, the original base of warabi mochi, though it is now scarce and costly.
