How to cook

How to choose

Stalks that are not too thick, bright green and taut, with a fresh cut end.

Storage

Parboil, peel, and keep submerged in water in the fridge. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Gunma
  • Osaka
  • Hokkaido

Good to know

One of the few vegetables native to Japan. The flower bud that appears in early spring is fuki-no-to.
