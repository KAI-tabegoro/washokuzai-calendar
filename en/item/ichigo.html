How to cook

How to choose

A deep green calyx that curls back, red right to the tip, and clearly defined seeds on the surface.

Storage

Refrigerate unwashed in the punnet. They bruise easily, so eat within two or three days. Wash only just before eating.

Main areas

  • Tochigi
  • Fukuoka
  • Kumamoto
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

The tip is sweeter than the stem end. Eating from the stem end keeps the last bite sweet.
