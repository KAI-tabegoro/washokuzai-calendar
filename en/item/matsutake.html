How to cook

How to choose

A thick, short stem with a cap not yet fully open, and a strong aroma.

Storage

Do not wash. Wrap in damp newspaper and refrigerate. The aroma fades, so use quickly.

Main areas

  • Nagano
  • Iwate
  • Okayama
  • Hiroshima

Good to know

It cannot be cultivated, so only wild mushrooms exist. Japanese production has fallen sharply and most sold is imported.
