How to cook

How to choose

Still soil-covered and moist, with clear banding, hard and firm.

Storage

Sensitive to both cold and dryness. Leave the soil on, wrap in newspaper and keep somewhere cool and dark at room temperature.

Main areas

  • Saitama
  • Chiba
  • Miyazaki
  • Ehime

Good to know

The sliminess clouds simmering liquid, so it is usually removed by salting or parboiling, though it also carries umami.
