How to cook

How to choose

Skin that has turned purple and is beginning to split, with white, glossy pulp.

Storage

Keeps a few days at room temperature. Eat split fruit soon. Refrigerate the skin.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Akita
  • Niigata
  • Nagano

Good to know

Yamagata grows most of Japan's crop. The pulp is sweet and full of seeds; the skin is faintly bitter and is treated there as a vegetable, stuffed or stir-fried. The name is said to come from the way the fruit splits open when ripe.
