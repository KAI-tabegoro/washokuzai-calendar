How to cook

How to choose

Deep, even colour and heavy in the hand. A yellow base means it is fully ripe.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. It gives off ethylene gas, so store it apart from other vegetables.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Nagano
  • Iwate
  • Yamagata

Good to know

A slightly greasy surface is called abura-agari and signals full ripeness. It is natural, not wax.
