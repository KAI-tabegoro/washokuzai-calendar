How to cook

How to choose

Evenly thick strands with a slippery coating and deep colour.

Storage

Rinse the salt from salted mozuku first. Keeps two to three days refrigerated, and freezes.

Main areas

  • Okinawa

Good to know

Almost all mozuku sold in Japan comes from Okinawa. Okinawan mozuku is the thick-stranded type with a firm bite.
