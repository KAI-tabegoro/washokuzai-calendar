How to cook

How to choose

Small unopened caps on straight white stems, with no droplets inside the bag.

Storage

Do not wash; refrigerate in the bag. Trimmed and separated, it also freezes.

Main areas

  • Nagano
  • Niigata
  • Fukuoka
  • Miyazaki

Good to know

The white, slender mushroom sold in shops is grown in darkness. Wild enokitake is brown with a broad cap and looks like an entirely different mushroom. Nagano produces more than half the national crop.
