How to cook

How to choose

A slippery surface and a belly tinged with yellow. The body should feel firm.

Storage

Normally handled live. Kabayaki keeps two to three days refrigerated, and freezes.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Aichi
  • Miyazaki
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

The wild season runs from autumn into winter, yet demand peaks in summer because of the Day of the Ox custom. Catches of glass eels have declined year by year.
