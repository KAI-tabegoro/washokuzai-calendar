How to cook

How to choose

Evenly sized holes and a white cut face. Heavy, with no blemishes.

Storage

Cover the cut face with film and refrigerate. It discolours in air; soaking in vinegared water keeps it white.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Tokushima
  • Aichi
  • Saga

Good to know

The tip end nearest the new shoot is tender, while the base end is stickier. Choose the part to suit the dish.
