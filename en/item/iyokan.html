How to cook

How to choose

A taut rind of deep reddish orange and a fruit that feels heavy.

Storage

Keep at cool room temperature. In a warm room, bag it and put it in the vegetable drawer.

Main areas

  • Ehime
  • Saga
  • Wakayama

Good to know

Found in Yamaguchi in the Meiji era, it took its name after spreading in Ehime, the old province of Iyo. Ehime now grows most of Japan's crop. The main type today is Miyauchi Iyokan, a bud sport found in Matsuyama in the 1950s.
