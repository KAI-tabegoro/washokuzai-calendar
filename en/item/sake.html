How to cook

How to choose

Deep, even flesh colour with fine white marbling. Fillet edges should not be ragged.

Storage

Pat fillets dry, wrap and refrigerate. Freezes well and is easy to handle.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Iwate
  • Miyagi
  • Aomori

Good to know

Fat fish caught just before returning to the rivers are called tokishirazu or keiji, and are treated as rarities.
