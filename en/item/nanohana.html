How to cook

How to choose

Tight, unopened buds and a fresh-looking cut at the stem.

Storage

Once the flowers open the flavour falls off. Wrap in damp newspaper, refrigerate upright and use quickly.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Tokushima
  • Kagawa
  • Mie

Good to know

The gentle bitterness is the point. Once it flowers the astringency grows, so it is picked in bud.
