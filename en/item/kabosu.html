How to choose

Deep green glossy skin, heavy in the hand and unblemished.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. Pressing the juice and freezing it is convenient.

Main areas

  • Oita

Good to know

More than ninety per cent of the national crop comes from Oita. It is a size larger than sudachi and yields more juice. Where sudachi is the speciality of Tokushima, kabosu is the citrus that represents Oita. It is shipped green, before it ripens yellow.
