How to cook

How to choose

A green calyx that curls back, even colour all over, and heavy for its size.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature before it is fully red, then refrigerate. Over-chilling dulls the flavour.

Main areas

Good to know

Heat makes the umami compound glutamate more available. Its role changes between raw and cooked dishes.
