How to cook

How to choose

Large, clear eyes. A vivid body colour and red gills.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Miyagi
  • Fukushima
  • Nagasaki
  • Hyogo

Good to know

It divides into three kinds, black, red and white rockfish, told apart by body colour. Because it appears early in the year it is also called the fish that announces spring.
