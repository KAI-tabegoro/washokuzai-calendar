How to choose

A thin, smooth rind and a fruit that feels heavy for its size, with a slender stem.

Storage

Bag it against drying and keep it in the vegetable drawer.

Main areas

  • Ehime
  • Wakayama
  • Hiroshima
  • Saga

Good to know

Japan's first tangor, registered in 1979 from a cross between the satsuma mandarin and the Trovita orange. It is named after Seiken temple in Shizuoka. It is the parent of Shiranui and Setoka, and much of Japan's fine citrus descends from it.
