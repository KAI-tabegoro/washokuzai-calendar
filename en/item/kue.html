How to cook

How to choose

A deep body colour and a firm body. Larger fish carry more fat and more flavour.

Storage

The firm flesh keeps well. Store whole packed in ice and cut only what you need. Steaks within two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Wakayama
  • Oita
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

Called ara in Kyushu, though the fish whose formal name is ara is an entirely different species, so care is needed. Some exceed a metre, and kue nabe in winter is regarded as the finest of dishes. It grows slowly, and wild fish are scarce.
