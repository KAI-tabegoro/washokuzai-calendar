How to cook

How to choose

Thick stalks with clear ribs and deep green leaves, and a cut end not yet dried.

Storage

Separate leaves from stalks, bag each and refrigerate. Use the leaves first.

Main areas

  • Nagano
  • Shizuoka
  • Fukuoka
  • Aichi

Good to know

Its distinctive scent comes from compounds including apiin, and it is used to take the edge off meat. The leaves are strongly scented too and go into soups and stir-fries. Tradition holds that Kato Kiyomasa brought it back from Korea, which earned it the old name kiyomasa-ninjin.
