How to cook

How to choose

Thick flesh with a translucent look, and an unbroken cuttlebone.

Storage

Remove the bone and innards before refrigerating. Sashimi-grade the same day. It also freezes.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Kumamoto
  • Saga
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

Its formal name is kaminari-ika, named for the lightning-like markings on its back. A cuttlefish with thick flesh that stays tender through cooking. Imported European cuttlefish also circulates in Japan under the same market name.
