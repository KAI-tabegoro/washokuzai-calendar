How to cook

How to choose

Clear stone-wall spotting, with good thickness and firmness through the body.

Storage

The firm flesh keeps well. Gut and refrigerate, using within two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Kagoshima
  • Kochi
  • Mie

Good to know

The black spots covering the body look like a stone wall, which is where the name comes from. Closely related to the striped beakfish; hybrids are called kindai. Old males develop a white snout and are called kuchijiro, white mouth, a fish anglers dream of landing.
