How to cook

How to choose

The base just beginning to split, colour right through, and a stem that is not wilted.

Storage

Spoils quickly; two or three days refrigerated. It does not ripen further, so it is at its best when bought.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Wakayama
  • Hyogo
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

The Japanese name means fruit without flowers, because it seems to fruit without blooming. In fact the flowers bloom inside the fruit.
