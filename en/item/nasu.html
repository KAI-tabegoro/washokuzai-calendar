How to cook

How to choose

Taut, glossy skin with deep colour. Sharp thorns on the calyx mean it is fresh.

Storage

Sensitive to cold. Wrap in newspaper and keep in the vegetable drawer. Below 5°C it deteriorates.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Kumamoto
  • Gunma
  • Ibaraki

Good to know

Many regional varieties exist: Kamo nasu in Kyoto, mizu nasu in Osaka, juzen nasu in Niigata, each different in shape and taste.
