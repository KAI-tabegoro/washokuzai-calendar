How to cook

How to choose

Dense, even bumps, deep colour, and firm to the touch.

Storage

Remove seeds and pith, wrap and refrigerate. Once cut it spoils quickly.

Main areas

  • Okinawa
  • Miyazaki
  • Kagoshima
  • Gunma

Good to know

The bitterness is concentrated in the seeds and pith. Salting or parboiling softens it. Ripe fruit turns yellow and loses its bitterness.
