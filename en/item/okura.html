How to cook

How to choose

Dense fine hairs and well-defined ridges. Oversized pods are tough.

Storage

Sensitive to cold. Wrap in newspaper and keep in the vegetable drawer. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Kochi
  • Okinawa
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

Rubbing the hairs off with salt improves the texture. Cutting before boiling lets the viscosity run out, so boil the pods whole.
