How to cook

How to choose

Thick and springy, not dried out, with a pale underside.

Storage

Fresh: refrigerate and use within a few days. Dried: keeps a long time at room temperature.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Kumamoto
  • Oita
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

A mushroom. Its Japanese name is written with characters meaning 'tree ear', after the ear-like shape in which it grows on wood. Japanese fresh wood ear is thick and markedly crunchy, and is treated as a different ingredient from the dried kind.
