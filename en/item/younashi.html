How to cook

How to choose

Undamaged fruit. It is hard before ripening; press around the stem, and give means it is ready.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature. Once soft, refrigerate and eat soon.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Niigata
  • Aomori
  • Nagano

Good to know

La France is the main variety in Japan, and Yamagata grows around seventy per cent of the crop. At harvest it is hard and undeveloped in flavour; only after about two weeks of ripening do the scent and sweetness emerge. That step has earned it a name as a fruit whose moment is hard to judge.
