How to cook

How to choose

A vivid scarlet body with clear eyes. A firm belly and intact scales.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The high fat content oxidises easily, so use within two days.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori
  • Iwate

Good to know

Its formal name is kichiji. Rausu kinki from Hokkaido is considered the finest. A deep-water fish with small catches, it is treated as a luxury.
