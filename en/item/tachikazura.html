How to cook

How to choose

Deep green with fine leaves set densely, and firm right to the tips.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Not edible; do not eat it.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Shizuoka
  • Kumamoto
  • Miyazaki

Good to know

Its formal name is hikage-no-kazura, a clubmoss whose fine leaves resemble cedar. In Japanese cooking it serves as a lining or decoration. It is not food, but is there to give colour and depth to a dish. It was once used in Shinto ritual decoration.
