How to choose

Translucent bodies with clearly visible black eyes, and no broken fish.

Storage

It loses freshness extremely quickly. Use the same day.

Good to know

A member of the icefish family, quite distinct from shirouo, which is a goby. The two are often confused, but shirauo turns cloudy white when it dies, while shirouo stays clear in life. Tokugawa Ieyasu is said to have been fond of it, and in Edo it marked the arrival of spring.
