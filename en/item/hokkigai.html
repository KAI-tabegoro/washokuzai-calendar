How to cook

How to choose

A thick shell, heavy in the hand, that reacts when touched.

Storage

In the shell it keeps two to three days refrigerated. Shucked meat should be blanched and eaten the same day.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori
  • Fukushima
  • Miyagi

Good to know

Its formal name is ubagai. Tomakomai in Hokkaido is famous for it and has made it the city shellfish. Raw it is pale, but blanching turns it vivid red.
