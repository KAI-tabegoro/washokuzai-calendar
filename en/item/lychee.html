How to cook

How to choose

Plump red skin with no damage, and some give when held.

Storage

The skin darkens as it dries. Bag and refrigerate, using within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Miyazaki
  • Kagoshima
  • Okinawa

Good to know

The tree-ripened lychee of Shintomi in Miyazaki is a brand picked only when fully ripe on the tree, and is regarded as a different fruit from imported frozen lychee. The scent fades quickly after picking, which is why imported fruit is harvested before full ripeness. It is known as the fruit favoured by Yang Guifei.
