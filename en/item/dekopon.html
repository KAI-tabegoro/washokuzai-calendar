How to choose

Deep colour and heavy in the hand. The size of the bump has no bearing on the taste.

Storage

Store somewhere cool and airy at room temperature. A bag helps prevent drying.

Main areas

  • Kumamoto
  • Ehime
  • Hiroshima
  • Wakayama

Good to know

The variety is shiranui. Only fruit meeting a standard of 13 degrees sugar or more and 1.0 or less citric acid may be sold as Dekopon, a registered trademark; the rest circulate as shiranui. The bump at the top is its signature.
