How to choose

A bright, glossy yellow rind and a fruit that feels heavy.

Storage

Bag it against drying and keep it in the vegetable drawer.

Main areas

  • Miyazaki
  • Kochi
  • Ehime

Good to know

Found by chance around 1820, in the Edo period, in a garden in Miyazaki. In Kochi it is called Konatsu and in Ehime New Summer Orange. Its white pith is soft and mildly sweet rather than bitter, and is eaten together with the flesh.
