How to cook

How to choose

A green cut face on the stem, with fine, closely set nodes on the rhizome. Short and thick is best.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Grate only what you need and keep the cut face intact.

Main areas

  • Shizuoka
  • Nagano
  • Iwate
  • Shimane

Good to know

Water wasabi is grown in spring-fed streams, field wasabi in soil. Utogi in Shizuoka is regarded as the birthplace of its cultivation. The aroma peaks about five minutes after grating and the heat fades with time, so it is grated just before use.
