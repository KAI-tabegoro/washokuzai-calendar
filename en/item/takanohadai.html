How to cook

How to choose

Deep, clear banding with distinct white spots on the tail. Choose winter fish.

Storage

Gut it promptly; left in, the innards pass on a seaweed-like smell. Refrigerate and use within two days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Shizuoka
  • Wakayama
  • Kochi

Good to know

Marked by broad oblique bands and white spots scattered across the tail. In Chinese-speaking regions this combination of stripes and spots is considered auspicious, and the fish is steamed whole for celebrations. Winter fish have a gentler aroma and firmer flesh.
