How to cook

How to choose

A silver sheen with unbroken pectoral fins. Clear eyes and a firm belly.

Storage

Loses freshness quickly; process the same day. Drying it allows long storage.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Shimane
  • Tottori
  • Miyazaki

Good to know

Called ago in Kyushu, where the grilled and dried fish makes a prized broth. It spreads its pectoral fins and glides several hundred metres over the sea.
