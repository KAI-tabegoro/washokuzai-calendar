How to cook

How to choose

A silver sheen and firm flesh. Fillets should be white and translucent.

Storage

The flesh is soft and breaks easily. Wrap and refrigerate, using within one or two days.

Main areas

  • Fukui
  • Kyoto
  • Nagasaki
  • Okayama

Good to know

The kanji combines fish and spring, yet in Kanto the winter kan-zawara is preferred. The season is understood differently by region.
