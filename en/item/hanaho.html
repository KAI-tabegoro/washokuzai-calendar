How to cook

How to choose

Flowers open over about the lower half of the spike, not further. The stem should look fresh.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Shizuoka
  • Kochi
  • Oita

Good to know

The flowering spike of the shiso plant. Both red and green shiso produce them, and the red is more often used with sashimi. Picked before the seeds set, its scent is finer than that of the seed spike. Grown under cover, it is shipped year-round.
