How to cook

How to choose

Thin, fine-textured skin with deep colour, a small calyx, and heavy in the hand.

Storage

Keep somewhere cool, dark and airy. If bought by the box, rotate the lower fruit, which spoils first.

Main areas

  • Wakayama
  • Ehime
  • Shizuoka
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

The white pith under the skin is nutritious too. Sunny, well-drained slopes make the best growing land.
