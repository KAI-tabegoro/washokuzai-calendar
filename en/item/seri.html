How to cook

How to choose

Deep green leaves with a strong aroma. Rooted bunches should have firm white roots.

Storage

Wrap in damp newspaper and refrigerate. The aroma fades, so use quickly.

Main areas

  • Miyagi
  • Ibaraki
  • Akita
  • Oita

Good to know

In Miyagi the roots are eaten too, in seri nabe. The aroma and bite of the roots are what people value.
