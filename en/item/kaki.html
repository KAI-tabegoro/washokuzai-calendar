How to cook

How to choose

A calyx that sits flush against the fruit, deep even colour, and heavy in the hand.

Storage

Refrigerate calyx-down. Ripen firm fruit at room temperature.

Main areas

  • Wakayama
  • Nara
  • Fukuoka
  • Gifu

Good to know

Astringent persimmons lose their astringency through drying or treatment with alcohol or carbon dioxide. The compound does not vanish; it simply stops dissolving.
