How to cook

How to choose

Deep green, glossy leaves with an even set of leaflets, and a strong scent.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Wakayama
  • Kyoto
  • Kochi
  • Tokushima

Good to know

The young shoots of the sansho pepper tree. Valued in Japanese cooking as the scent that announces spring. A light clap between the palms just before use brings out the aroma. Quite different in part and season from the sansho peppercorn.
