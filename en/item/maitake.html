How to cook

How to choose

Thick caps in a deep brown, on a firm cluster with white stems.

Storage

Do not wash. Wrap in paper and refrigerate. Torn into portions, it also freezes.

Main areas

  • Niigata
  • Shizuoka
  • Gunma
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

The name is said to come from dancing for joy at finding one. Wild maitake was scarce enough that it was once exchanged for its own weight in silver. It contains an enzyme that breaks down protein, so adding it to chawanmushi keeps the egg from setting.
