How to cook

How to choose

Thin, bright green pods with upright tendrils, and beans not yet bulging.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Bag and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Aichi
  • Fukushima
  • Wakayama

Good to know

Eaten whole they are saya-endo; the shelled beans are green peas; snap peas can be eaten either way.
