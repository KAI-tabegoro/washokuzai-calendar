How to cook

How to choose

Pale brown, moist skin with few, low bumps at the base. Green tips mean it has grown too far and will be bitter.

Storage

Bitterness builds from the moment it is dug, so parboil the same day with rice bran and dried chilli. Once boiled, keep submerged in water in the fridge for four to five days.

Main areas

  • Kyoto
  • Fukuoka
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

Shirako bamboo shoots from Otokuni in Kyoto are grown under straw and mounded soil, which keeps them white and tender.
