How to cook

How to choose

Thick, deep green leaves on stout straight stems, free of damage.

Storage

Fresh leaves do not keep. Pickle them at once, or refrigerate and use within two days.

Main areas

  • Nagano

Good to know

Said to have begun at Nozawa Onsen in Nagano, where tradition holds that a priest of Kenmeiji brought the seed back from Kyoto. Frost sweetens it, so it is harvested and pickled around the first frosts. The plants grow to nearly a metre.
