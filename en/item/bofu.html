How to cook

How to choose

Vivid red stems with fresh, deep green leaves, unbroken.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Shizuoka
  • Chiba
  • Aichi
  • Kochi

Good to know

What reaches the market is the cultivated iso-bofu, marked by its red stems. The wild hama-bofu of the seashore has a stronger scent and is prized for sashimi, but little of it is gathered. The Japanese name means warding off colds, and it has long been treated as a medicinal herb.
