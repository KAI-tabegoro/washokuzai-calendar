How to cook

How to choose

Even colour all over with a strong aroma, and symmetrical either side of the seam.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature and chill two or three hours before eating. Over-chilling dulls the sweetness.

Main areas

  • Yamanashi
  • Fukushima
  • Nagano
  • Yamagata

Good to know

The flesh is very soft and bruises easily. Do not press with a finger to test it; that spot will spoil.
