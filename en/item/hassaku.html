How to choose

Heavy in the hand with taut skin, and a stem that has not dried.

Storage

Store somewhere cool, dark and airy. It dries out, so a bag helps.

Main areas

  • Wakayama
  • Hiroshima
  • Ehime
  • Tokushima

Good to know

It is said to have been found at a temple on Innoshima in Hiroshima. The segment membrane is thick, so each is peeled before eating. The gentle bitterness comes from naringin, which gives hassaku its particular character. It is stored for a period after harvest to let the acidity settle before shipping.
