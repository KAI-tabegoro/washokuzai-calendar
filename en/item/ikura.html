How to cook

How to choose

Evenly sized eggs that are taut and glossy. Avoid broken ones.

Storage

Soy-marinated roe keeps three to four days refrigerated. Portion and freeze for longer.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Iwate
  • Miyagi
  • Aomori

Good to know

Loose eggs are ikura; the whole egg sac is sujiko. The word comes from Russian for fish roe.
