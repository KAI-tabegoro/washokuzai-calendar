How to cook

How to choose

The adductor muscle should be translucent and springy. Live scallops should close when touched.

Storage

The adductor keeps one or two days refrigerated. It freezes, but loses liquid on thawing.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori
  • Iwate
  • Miyagi

Good to know

Hokkaido and Aomori account for most national production. Seabed-sown and hanging-culture scallops are said to taste different.
