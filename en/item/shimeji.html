How to cook

How to choose

Caps not yet fully open, with thick white stems and a firm cluster.

Storage

Do not wash. Wrap in paper and refrigerate. Trimmed and separated, it also freezes in portions.

Main areas

  • Nagano
  • Niigata
  • Fukuoka
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

The shimeji of the saying about matsutake for aroma and shimeji for flavour is the wild honshimeji. Most of what reaches the market is cultivated bunashimeji, a different species. Honshimeji is difficult to cultivate and remains scarce.
