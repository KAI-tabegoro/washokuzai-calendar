How to cook

How to choose

Clearly raised netting and a fruit that feels heavy for its size. It is ready when the base gives a little under the thumb.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature. Once it smells fragrant, chill two or three hours before eating. Too cold and the sweetness is lost.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Ibaraki
  • Kumamoto
  • Yamagata

Good to know

A melon with orange flesh, coloured by the carotene it carries. It smells stronger than the green-fleshed type and tastes sweeter. Hokkaido fruit peaks in July and August, later than the green type.
