How to cook

How to choose

Thick, glossy dark brown shells, tightly closed.

Storage

Purge sand in weak salt water of about 1%. Freezing is said to increase the umami compounds.

Main areas

  • Shimane
  • Aomori
  • Ibaraki
  • Hokkaido

Good to know

Lake Shinji in Shimane is the largest source. Both the summer doyo-shijimi and the winter kan-shijimi are considered at their best.
