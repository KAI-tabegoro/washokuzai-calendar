How to choose

Deep colour with a glossy, smooth surface. A narrower core means more tender flesh.

Storage

Pat dry, wrap in newspaper and refrigerate standing upright. Sensitive to humidity.

Good to know

Beta-carotene is concentrated near the skin, and is absorbed better when eaten with oil.
