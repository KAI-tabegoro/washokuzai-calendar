How to cook

How to choose

A deep body colour with clear bands. Steaks should be translucent with taut skin.

Storage

The firm flesh keeps relatively well. Pat steaks dry, wrap and refrigerate, using within two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Mie
  • Chiba
  • Kochi

Good to know

Seven bands across the body give the fish its English name. They are clear in young fish and fade with age. Treated as a luxury fish, and also farmed.
