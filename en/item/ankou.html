How to cook

How to choose

Translucent flesh with a slippery coating. The liver should be white and firm.

Storage

Separate the parts and refrigerate. Use within one or two days.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Fukushima
  • Yamaguchi
  • Shimane

Good to know

A saying pairs pufferfish in the west with monkfish in the east. Almost everything but the bones is eaten, divided into what are called the seven tools.
