How to choose

A firm body with clear eyes. Fish from clean waters are best.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate, using within two days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki

  • Mie
  • Kagawa

Good to know

It changes name as it grows, which is said to be the origin of a Japanese phrase for coming to the end of something. Its roe, salted and dried, becomes karasumi, prized as a speciality of Nagasaki. Winter fish are regarded as free of muddiness and at their best.
