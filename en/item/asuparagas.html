How to cook

How to choose

Tight, straight tips and a fresh-looking cut end. Thicker spears are more tender and sweeter.

Storage

Refrigerate standing upright. Laid flat, the tips try to turn upward and the spears bend.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Saga
  • Nagano
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

Grown without sunlight it becomes white asparagus. The same variety changes colour with the growing method.
