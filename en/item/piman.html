How to cook

How to choose

Glossy, taut skin with rounded shoulders, and a fresh-looking cut at the stem.

Storage

Pat dry, bag and refrigerate. Keeps about a week.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Miyazaki
  • Kochi
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

Left to ripen it turns red, growing sweeter and less bitter. A different variety from bell peppers.
