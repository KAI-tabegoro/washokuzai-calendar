How to cook

How to choose

Thick, deep green leaves with a vivid red root end, and stems that are not too thick.

Storage

Wrap in damp newspaper and refrigerate standing upright. Sensitive to drying.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Saitama
  • Gunma
  • Ibaraki

Good to know

The astringent compounds reduce when boiled and soaked in water. Winter spinach sweetens and is sold as chijimi horenso.
