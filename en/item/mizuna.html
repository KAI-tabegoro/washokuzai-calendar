How to cook

How to choose

Deep green leaves with slender white stems. A firm head with no damage.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper and refrigerate standing upright. Use within three or four days.

Main areas

  • Kyoto
  • Ibaraki
  • Fukuoka
  • Shiga

Good to know

One of the Kyoto vegetables, said to be named for growing on water and soil alone, without fertiliser. Also called kyona in the Kansai region. The crisp sound it makes in a hot pot gave the dish harihari nabe its name.
