How to cook

How to choose

Buds still tight and unopened, in a vivid green and undamaged.

Storage

The bitterness sharpens once it opens. Wrap in damp paper, refrigerate and use soon.

Main areas

  • Akita
  • Gunma
  • Hokkaido
  • Nagano

Good to know

The flower bud of the butterbur, pushing up as the snow melts, a herald of spring. Its particular bitterness comes from alkaloids and polyphenols, and is thought of as the taste that wakes the body from winter. The bitterness strengthens once the bud opens, so it is used while still tight.
