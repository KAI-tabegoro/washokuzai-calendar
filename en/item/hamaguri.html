How to cook

How to choose

Thick, glossy shells, tightly closed.

Storage

Purge sand in salt water before cooking. Keeps two to three days refrigerated.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Ibaraki
  • Mie
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

Because each shell only fits its own pair, it symbolises a lasting marriage and appears at weddings and the Doll Festival.
