How to cook

How to choose

Crisp markings on the back and a silvery-white belly. Firm flesh, and a rounded body means plenty of fat.

Storage

Loses freshness especially fast; process it the same day. Freeze once before eating raw.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Ibaraki
  • Shizuoka
  • Fukui

Good to know

Richest from autumn into winter, known then as aki-saba and kan-saba. Branded lines such as Seki-saba and Matsuwa-saba are well known.
