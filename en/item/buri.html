How to cook

How to choose

A bright red bloodline and firm flesh. In fillets, look for white fat marbled evenly through.

Storage

Pat fillets dry, wrap and refrigerate. Sashimi-grade should be eaten the same day.

Main areas

  • Toyama
  • Ishikawa
  • Nagasaki
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

A fish that changes name as it grows. In Kansai: tsubasu, hamachi, mejiro, buri. In Kanto: wakashi, inada, warasa, buri.
