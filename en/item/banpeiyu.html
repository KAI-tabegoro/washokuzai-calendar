How to choose

Heavy in the hand with glossy skin and a strong scent.

Storage

It holds about a month at room temperature. It can be displayed for its scent before eating.

Main areas

  • Kumamoto

Good to know

At twenty-five centimetres across and two kilograms, it is among the largest citrus in the world. Yatsushiro in Kumamoto is the main growing area, and the peel reaches two to three centimetres thick. That peel, candied as zabon-zuke, is a local speciality, so both flesh and skin are put to use.
