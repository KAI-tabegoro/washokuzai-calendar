How to cook

How to choose

Blooms not yet fully open, with vivid colour and a fresh cut on the stem.

Storage

Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Fukuoka
  • Okinawa
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

The single chrysanthemum laid beside sashimi, grown for the table. Pulling the petals into the soy sauce is the proper use, and the flower is held to have an antibacterial effect. A different variety from the large edible chrysanthemum; this one is mainly for colour.
