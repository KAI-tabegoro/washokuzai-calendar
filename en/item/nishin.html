How to cook

How to choose

A blue-sheened body that feels firm, with clear eyes and a solid belly.

Storage

The high fat content spoils quickly. Gut, refrigerate and use by the next day.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido

Good to know

It crowds the Hokkaido coast to spawn in spring, which earned it the name spring-herald fish. Its roe, kazunoko, is essential at New Year. Once caught in vast numbers, the dried fish carried to Kyoto by the Kitamae ships gave rise to nishin soba.
