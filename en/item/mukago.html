How to cook

How to choose

Even in size, firm, with taut skin and no wrinkles.

Storage

A few days somewhere cool and dark. For longer, boil and freeze.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Nagano
  • Yamagata
  • Iwate

Good to know

Small bulbils that form where the leaves meet the vine of the yam plant. Fallen to the ground they sprout and in time become yams. They taste and smell exactly like yam, and appear for only a short spell in autumn. They must be picked by hand, so little reaches the market.
