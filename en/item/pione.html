How to cook

How to choose

Large berries coloured deep purple-black with a white bloom, on a green stem.

Storage

Bag unwashed and refrigerate. Keep the bunch intact.

Main areas

  • Okayama
  • Yamanashi
  • Nagano
  • Yamagata

Good to know

Bred in Shizuoka by crossing Kyoho with Cannon Hall Muscat. The berries are larger than Kyoho, and seedless-treated fruit is sold as New Pione. Okayama is the main growing area, and the fruit is both high in sugar and strongly aromatic.
