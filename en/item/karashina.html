How to cook

How to choose

Deep green leaves that feel taut and show no wilting, on slender tender stems.

Storage

Wrap in damp paper and refrigerate standing upright. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Fukuoka
  • Oita
  • Kochi
  • Niigata

Good to know

The sharp heat comes from allyl isothiocyanate, the same compound found in wasabi and daikon. The seeds are the source of Japanese mustard. Takana and katsuo-na belong to the same group, and in Kyushu they are widely eaten as pickles.
