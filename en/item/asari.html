How to cook

How to choose

Shells closed, or closing immediately when touched, with clear markings.

Storage

Purge sand in about 3% salt water, the same as seawater. Fresh water kills them. Freezes well.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Shizuoka
  • Fukuoka
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

Always purge in salt water. Fresh water weakens the clams, so salt must be dissolved in.
