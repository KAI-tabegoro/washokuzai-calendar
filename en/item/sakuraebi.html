How to cook

How to choose

A vivid cherry-blossom pink with the shape intact. Dried shrimp should be deeply red.

Storage

Raw shrimp the same day; boiled keeps two days refrigerated. Dried, seal and refrigerate or freeze.

Main areas

  • Shizuoka

Good to know

Suruga Bay is the only place in Japan where it is fished commercially. Fishing is strictly limited to two seasons, spring and autumn, to protect the stock. Its translucent pink body carries light organs, and it lives at depths of two to three hundred metres.
