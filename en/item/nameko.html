How to cook

How to choose

Caps not yet fully open, with clear rather than cloudy slime, on short firm stems.

Storage

It spoils quickly. Refrigerate in the bag and use within two days. It also freezes.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Nagano
  • Niigata
  • Fukushima

Good to know

The slipperiness comes from polysaccharides, and washing it away too thoroughly loses the point of the mushroom. Wild nameko grows on fallen beech and opens a broad cap. Cultivated ones are picked young and stay small. Yamagata grows about a third of the national crop.
