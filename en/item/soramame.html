How to cook

How to choose

Taut, glossy pods still carrying fine hairs, with evenly swollen beans.

Storage

The flavour falls away fast once shelled. Keep them in the pod in the fridge and shell just before use.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Chiba
  • Ibaraki
  • Ehime

Good to know

It is said to be good for only three days; freshness decides the taste. The Japanese name comes from the pods pointing skyward as they grow.
