How to cook

How to choose

Deep green and glossy, with a fresh cut at the stem and no wrinkles.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Wakayama
  • Miyazaki
  • Tokushima

Good to know

The tip resembles a lion's head, which is where the name comes from. Roughly one in ten is fiercely hot. Short water or sunlight stresses the plant and raises the pungent compounds, and those with few seeds, light in the hand, tend to be the hot ones.
