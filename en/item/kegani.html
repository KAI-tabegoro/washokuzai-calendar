How to choose

Heavy for its size, with a hard shell and dense hair.

Storage

Boiled crab keeps two to three days refrigerated.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido

Good to know

Seasons differ around Hokkaido, so it is available almost year-round as the source shifts. Known less for its meat than for its rich innards.
