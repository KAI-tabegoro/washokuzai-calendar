How to cook

How to choose

Thick, firm stems covered in down, with the tips still curled.

Storage

Fresh shoots must be de-bittered before refrigerating. Dried ones keep a long time at room temperature.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Akita
  • Niigata
  • Fukushima

Good to know

It cannot be eaten raw; the bitterness has to be drawn out with baking soda. Dried zenmai keeps well and is rehydrated for simmered dishes and namul. Thicker stems and a covering of down tell it apart from warabi.
