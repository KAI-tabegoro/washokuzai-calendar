How to cook

How to choose

Spring cabbage should be loosely packed and light; winter cabbage firm and heavy. The cut end should not be discoloured.

Storage

Cut out the core, pack the cavity with damp kitchen paper, bag it and refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Gunma
  • Aichi
  • Chiba
  • Nagano

Good to know

Spring cabbage is tender and suits raw dishes; winter cabbage has thick leaves for cooking. The same vegetable behaves differently by season.
