How to choose

Large berries in a vivid green, firm to the touch, on a thick green stem.

Storage

Bag unwashed and refrigerate. Keep the bunch intact and wash just before eating.

Main areas

  • Yamanashi
  • Nagano
  • Yamagata
  • Okayama

Good to know

What dominates the market now is Shine Muscat, a relatively new variety registered in 2006. It is seedless, eaten skin and all, and high in sugar. Before it, the glasshouse-grown Muscat of Alexandria was the luxury grape of Japan.
