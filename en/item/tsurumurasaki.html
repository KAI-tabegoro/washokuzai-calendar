How to cook

How to choose

Thick, deep green leaves on stems that are not too coarse, with a fresh cut end.

Storage

Wrap in damp paper and refrigerate standing upright. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Fukushima
  • Miyagi
  • Yamagata
  • Tokushima

Good to know

A climbing vegetable native to tropical Asia, in a different family from spinach. It stands heat well and appears when leafy greens are scarce in summer. The slipperiness and earthy scent are distinctive, and overcooking makes the slipperiness too pronounced. Varieties come with red or green stems.
