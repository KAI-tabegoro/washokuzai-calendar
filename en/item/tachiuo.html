How to cook

How to choose

The silver skin should be intact and glossy. The wider the body, the more fat.

Storage

The silver skin comes off easily, so handle gently. Refrigerate for one or two days.

Main areas

  • Wakayama
  • Oita
  • Ehime
  • Nagasaki

Good to know

The silver coating is guanine, once used to make imitation pearls.
