How to choose

Akami should be vivid with fine, even grain. Toro should show parallel lines of fat.

Storage

Eat the same day when refrigerated. If freezing, freeze fast and thaw only what you will eat.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Hokkaido
  • Nagasaki
  • Wakayama

Good to know

Pole-and-line fishing at Oma in Aomori is famous. Full-cycle aquaculture is now established, easing pressure on wild stocks.
