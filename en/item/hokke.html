How to cook

How to choose

For dried fish, thick flesh that is not over-dried. Fresh fish should have clear eyes and a firm body.

Storage

Fresh fish spoils extremely fast; eat the same day. Dried fish freezes well.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido

Good to know

Because it spoils so fast, it is sold almost only dried outside the producing region. Rausu in Hokkaido is well known.
