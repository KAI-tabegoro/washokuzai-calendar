How to cook

How to choose

Thick white scales drawn tightly together. Bulbs packed in sawdust are best.

Storage

Keep in the sawdust somewhere cool and dark. Sensitive to drying, so wrap in newspaper.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Niigata
  • Toyama
  • Nagano

Good to know

The bulbs of the tiger lily and its relatives. It takes about six years from seed bulb to shipping, which makes it a laborious crop, and more than ninety per cent comes from Hokkaido. The point is not to overcook it, or bitterness comes through.
