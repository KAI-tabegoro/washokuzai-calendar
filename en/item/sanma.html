How to cook

How to choose

A faintly yellow snout and a dark blue sheen on the back. A firm belly, and the head should not droop when held.

Storage

Spoils quickly, so eat the same day. Remove the guts before freezing.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Iwate
  • Miyagi

Good to know

Once a cheap everyday fish, poor catches in recent years have pushed prices up. Changing migration routes and rising sea temperatures are blamed.
