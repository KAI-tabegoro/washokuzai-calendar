How to cook

How to choose

A thick, taut siphon that contracts when touched, with a glossy dark skin.

Storage

Loses freshness quickly. Scald, peel, refrigerate and use the same day.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Mie
  • Chiba
  • Oita

Good to know

Its formal name is mirukui. The name comes from the sea algae called miru that attaches to the siphon. Catches are very small, making it the most expensive of shellfish. The part eaten is the siphon.
