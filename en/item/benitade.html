How to cook

How to choose

Vivid red stems with both seed leaves intact, and no wilting.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Shizuoka
  • Osaka
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

The blanched sprouts of the water pepper plant. It is the tade of the proverb about there being no accounting for taste, and its heat is held to suppress the smell of raw fish. Served with sashimi not only for colour but for its antibacterial effect.
