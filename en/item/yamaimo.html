How to cook

How to choose

Even thickness with few root hairs, and a cut face still white.

Storage

Whole, wrap in newspaper and keep somewhere cool and dark. Cover a cut face with film and refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Hokkaido
  • Nagano
  • Gunma

Good to know

Nagaimo holds more water and is loose enough to eat raw. Yamatoimo is far stickier, enough to lift with chopsticks when grated. Wild jinenjo is the stickiest of all and expensive. Choose by how much stickiness the dish needs.
