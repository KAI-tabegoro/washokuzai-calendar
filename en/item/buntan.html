How to cook

How to choose

A taut, glossy rind and a fruit that feels heavy, not shrivelled around the stem.

Storage

Kept somewhere cool and airy, it lasts one to two weeks at room temperature. Refrigerate peeled flesh and eat it soon.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

Kochi grows almost all of Japan's crop. The fruit is harvested in winter and stored for a while so the acidity mellows and the sweetness and fragrance develop before it is shipped. Legend says the name comes from the captain of a Chinese ship that drifted ashore in Kagoshima in the Edo period. Greenhouse fruit also appears in autumn.
