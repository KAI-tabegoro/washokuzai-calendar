How to cook

How to choose

Dense brown silk, deep green husks, and kernels packed right to the tip.

Storage

Sugars start falling the moment it is picked. Cook the same day, or boil and refrigerate if not eating at once.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Chiba
  • Ibaraki
  • Gunma

Good to know

The number of silks equals the number of kernels. More silk means a better-filled ear.
