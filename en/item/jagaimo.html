How to cook

How to choose

Taut skin with no sprouts. Avoid any with a green tinge.

Storage

Light turns them green, so store in the dark. Keeping them with an apple slows sprouting.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Kagoshima
  • Nagasaki
  • Ibaraki

Good to know

Green patches and sprouts contain solanine and must be removed thoroughly before cooking.
