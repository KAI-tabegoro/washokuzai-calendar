How to cook

How to choose

Translucent, springy flesh. Buy it already processed.

Storage

Refrigerate for one or two days. Thin-sliced sashimi should be eaten the same day.

Main areas

  • Yamaguchi
  • Fukuoka
  • Ishikawa
  • Nagasaki

Good to know

Preparation requires a specific licence. Shimonoseki in Yamaguchi is the trading centre. Preparing it without a licence is illegal.The milt, known as shirako, is prized as a winter delicacy and fetches high prices.
