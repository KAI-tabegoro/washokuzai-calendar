How to cook

How to choose

Even fine hairs, with slight give when pressed lightly. Hard fruit is unripe.

Storage

Bag hard fruit with an apple to speed ripening. Refrigerate once soft.

Main areas

  • Ehime
  • Fukuoka
  • Wakayama
  • Kanagawa

Good to know

Japanese kiwi is at its best from winter into spring. It needs ripening, so leave hard fruit at room temperature.
