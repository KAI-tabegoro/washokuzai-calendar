How to cook

How to choose

Evenly sized berries with a uniform white bloom, on a thick green stem.

Storage

Bag unwashed and refrigerate. Keep the bunch intact and cut off only what you will eat.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Yamanashi
  • Osaka
  • Shimane

Good to know

An American variety brought to Japan in the Meiji era. The gibberellin treatment that makes it seedless was established in Japan and became the basis for seedless grape growing thereafter. Small but high in sugar, and among the earliest to reach the market.
