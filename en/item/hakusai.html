How to cook

How to choose

Heavy in the hand and tightly packed, with a fresh white cut face. Small black spots do not affect quality.

Storage

Whole heads keep wrapped in newspaper standing upright somewhere cool and dark. Once cut it spoils from the cut face, so refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Nagano
  • Hokkaido
  • Gunma

Good to know

Exposed to frost it stores sugar to avoid freezing, so the colder it gets the sweeter it becomes.
