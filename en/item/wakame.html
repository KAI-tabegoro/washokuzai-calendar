How to cook

How to choose

Fresh wakame should be deeply coloured and slippery. Dried wakame should be jet black and glossy.

Storage

Salting fresh wakame makes it keep longer. Store dried wakame sealed at room temperature.

Main areas

  • Iwate
  • Miyagi
  • Tokushima
  • Hyogo

Good to know

Sanriku and Naruto are the two great sources. Naruto wakame grows in fierce currents and has a notably firm bite.
