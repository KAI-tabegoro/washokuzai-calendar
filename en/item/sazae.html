How to cook

How to choose

A tightly closed operculum, heavy for its size, and it retracts when touched.

Storage

Keep alive in the fridge wrapped in damp newspaper. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Yamaguchi
  • Mie
  • Ishikawa

Good to know

Grown in rough open water it develops long horns; in calm bays the horns are short or absent. The environment shows in the shape.
