How to cook

How to choose

A bright silver body. Fish with bellies full of roe are especially prized. Scales intact.

Storage

It spoils quickly. Refrigerate and use the same or the next day. Grill frozen fish from frozen.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido

Good to know

A species found only in Japan, running up a handful of rivers on the Pacific coast of Hokkaido. It is caught only during the spawning season in October and November. Most of what circulates as shishamo is capelin from the North Atlantic, a different fish in a different family.
