How to cook

How to choose

Heavy for its size, with a hard shell and all legs intact.

Storage

Boiled crab keeps two to three days refrigerated. Cook raw crab the same day.

Main areas

  • Hyogo
  • Tottori
  • Fukui
  • Ishikawa

Good to know

Each region has its own brand name: Echizen-gani in Fukui, Taiza-gani in Kyoto, Matsuba-gani in the San'in area. The season runs from early November to late March.
