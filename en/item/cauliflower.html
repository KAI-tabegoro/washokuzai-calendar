How to cook

How to choose

White, tightly packed curds, heavy in the hand, with fresh green outer leaves.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. It also freezes once boiled.

Main areas

  • Tokushima
  • Ibaraki
  • Aichi
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

A variant of broccoli; the curd stays white because the leaves are tied over it to keep out the light. Its vitamin C survives cooking better than most. Orange, purple and pale green varieties exist, and the spiralled romanesco belongs to the same group.
