How to cook

How to choose

Clean white shells that make no sound when shaken, and heavy in the hand.

Storage

Keep in the shell in the fridge; the kernels shrink if they dry. Shelled nuts freeze well.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Fukuoka
  • Oita
  • Niigata

Good to know

Eating too many can cause poisoning, and children need particular care. Even adults should avoid large quantities at once.
