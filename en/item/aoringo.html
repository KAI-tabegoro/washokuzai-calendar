How to cook

How to choose

Vivid yellow-green skin that feels taut, heavy in the hand, with a good scent.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. It gives off ethylene, so store it apart from other vegetables.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Nagano
  • Iwate
  • Yamagata

Good to know

The representative variety is Orin, bred in Aomori, whose name means king of apples. Compared with red apples such as Fuji, it is gentler in acidity and stronger in scent. Shinano Gold and Toki are also treated as green apples.
