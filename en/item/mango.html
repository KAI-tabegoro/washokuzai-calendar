How to cook

How to choose

Red colour over the whole fruit and a sweet aroma. Slight give when pressed means it is ready.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature and, once the aroma strengthens, chill two or three hours before eating. Over-chilling dulls the sweetness.

Main areas

  • Miyazaki
  • Okinawa
  • Kagoshima
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

Taiyo no Tamago from Miyazaki may only be named as such if it meets strict standards including a sugar content of 15 degrees or more. The fruit is left to ripen on the tree and caught in nets as it falls, so it carries the flavour of full tree ripening.
