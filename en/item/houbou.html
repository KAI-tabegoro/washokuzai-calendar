How to cook

How to choose

A vivid red body with brilliant blue-green inside the pectoral fins. A firm body.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The firm flesh holds for two to three days.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Ibaraki
  • Nagasaki
  • Fukushima

Good to know

The inside of the pectoral fins is a brilliant blue-green, and spread open they look like butterfly wings. The name is said to come from the booming sound it makes with its swim bladder. It walks along the seabed on finger-like rays beneath the pectoral fins.
