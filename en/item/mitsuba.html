How to cook

How to choose

Fresh-looking leaves on straight, unbroken stems, with a strong scent.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Root-on bunches stand in water in the fridge. Once chopped, use the same day.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Aichi
  • Ibaraki
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

Named for the three leaflets on each stem. Three growing methods give root mitsuba, cut mitsuba and thread mitsuba, each used differently. One of the few vegetables native to Japan. Overheating drives off the scent, so it goes in last.
