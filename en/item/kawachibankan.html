How to cook

How to choose

A taut, pale yellow rind and a fruit that feels heavy for its size.

Storage

Keep at cool room temperature. In summer, bag it and put it in the vegetable drawer.

Main areas

  • Ehime
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

A relative of the pomelo, found around 1905 in Kawachi, Kumamoto (now part of Kumamoto city). The fruit is left on the tree through the winter and harvested from spring into summer. Its light taste has earned it the name "Japanese grapefruit". It is sold under different names depending on where it is grown.
