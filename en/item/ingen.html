How to cook

How to choose

Bright, glossy and firm all the way to the tip.

Storage

Bag it against drying and keep it in the vegetable drawer. It does not keep long; use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Kagoshima
  • Fukushima
  • Okinawa

Good to know

The Japanese name, ingen, comes from the tradition that the Chinese monk Ingen brought the bean to Japan in the Edo period. Most varieties today are stringless. Because it can be harvested three times a year, it is also called sandomame, "three-times bean", in the Kansai region.
