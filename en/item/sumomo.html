How to cook

How to choose

A white bloom on the skin, with some give when held, and a good scent.

Storage

Ripen firm fruit at room temperature and refrigerate once it softens.

Main areas

  • Yamanashi
  • Nagano
  • Wakayama
  • Yamagata

Good to know

Known in English as the Japanese plum, with varieties including Oishi Wase, Soldam and Taiyo. Its Japanese name is said to come from being gentler in acidity than ume and therefore edible raw. The acidity sits near the skin, so eating it unpeeled sharpens the flavour.
