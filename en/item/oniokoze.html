How to cook

How to choose

A vivid body colour and a firm body. Live fish are best.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Oita
  • Hyogo
  • Yamaguchi

Good to know

The dorsal spines carry a strong venom and must be handled with care; they are always cut off before cooking. Despite its appearance the flesh is refined, and it is prized as a summer luxury. The skin is also eaten, briefly scalded.
