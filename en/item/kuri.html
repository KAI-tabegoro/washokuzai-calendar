How to cook

How to choose

A taut, glossy outer shell, heavy in the hand. Small holes mean insect damage.

Storage

A week in the chiller drawer increases the sweetness. For longer storage, boil and freeze.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Kumamoto
  • Ehime
  • Gifu

Good to know

Stored at around 0°C the starch converts to sugar, and the sweetness is said to increase several times over.
