How to cook

How to choose

A firm body with a white belly and clear eyes. Pre-cut fillets should not be broken up.

Storage

It is hardy and holds its freshness well. Once bone-cut, use it the same day.

Main areas

  • Tokushima
  • Hyogo
  • Ehime
  • Oita

Good to know

Its many fine bones are dealt with by honekiri, a knife technique that makes twenty-four to twenty-six cuts per inch while leaving the skin intact. In Kyoto its season falls around the Gion Festival, which is sometimes called the hamo festival, and it is central to the city's summer cooking.
