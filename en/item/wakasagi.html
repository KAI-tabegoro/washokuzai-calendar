How to cook

How to choose

A translucent silver body with clear eyes, and flesh that has not broken up.

Storage

Small and quick to spoil. Refrigerate and use the same day. It also freezes once prepared.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori
  • Ibaraki
  • Nagano

Good to know

Fishing for it through a hole cut in lake ice is a sight of winter in Japan. The bones are soft and the innards only gently bitter, so it is eaten whole without preparation. It lives in both sea and lake, and lake fish complete their lives within a year.
