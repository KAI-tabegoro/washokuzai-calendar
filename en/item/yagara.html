How to cook

How to choose

A vivid scarlet body that feels firm, with clear eyes.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The firm flesh holds for two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Kochi
  • Shizuoka
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

Two kinds exist, red and blue cornetfish; almost all eaten is the red. Its long body and long snout mean most of the fish is head and tail, so there is little to eat, yet its refined flavour makes it a luxury.
