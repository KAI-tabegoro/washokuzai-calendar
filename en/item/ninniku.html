How to cook

How to choose

White, dry outer skin drawn tight, heavy in the hand, with no sprouting.

Storage

Hang somewhere cool, dark and airy. Refrigerating or freezing also holds off sprouting.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Kagawa
  • Miyazaki
  • Iwate

Good to know

Aomori grows around seventy per cent of the national crop, and its Fukuchi White has notably large, pale cloves. Cutting or grating sets enzymes working to create the characteristic aroma, so chop it fine when you want that scent to carry. Black garlic is the same bulb aged until the sugars concentrate.
