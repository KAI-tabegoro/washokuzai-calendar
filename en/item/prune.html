How to choose

Deep purple with plenty of white bloom left on, and taut skin.

Storage

Ripen firm fruit at room temperature; once ripe, refrigerate and eat soon.

Main areas

  • Nagano
  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori

Good to know

Prunes are European plum varieties that can be dried whole without fermenting. In Japan, Nagano grows most of the crop and ships it fresh. The white bloom on the skin is made by the fruit itself and is a sign of freshness.
