How to choose

A thick green stem and taut, glossy fruit. Deeper colour means riper.

Storage

Over-chilling dulls the flavour; chill only just before eating. They do not keep, so eat within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Hokkaido
  • Yamanashi
  • Aomori

Good to know

Yamagata grows most of the national crop. The Sato Nishiki variety was bred by Sato Eisuke and carries his name.
