How to cook

How to choose

Plump white bulbs with firm, upright leaves.

Storage

Wrap in damp paper and refrigerate. The scent fades fast, so use it soon.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Nagano
  • Tokushima
  • Oita

Good to know

A wild relative of onion and garlic. Both the bulb and the leaves are eaten. It has a sharp bite and a strong scent, and is most often eaten raw with miso. Its leaves resemble those of the daffodil, which is poisonous, so care is needed when foraging.
