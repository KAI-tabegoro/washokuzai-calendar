How to choose

Small but heavy in the hand, deep red, with a lively calyx.

Storage

Room temperature or refrigerated. Chilling just before eating lifts the sweetness.

Main areas

  • Shizuoka
  • Kochi
  • Kumamoto
  • Nagano

Good to know

Not a variety but a method. Water is held back severely, which raises the sugar content above eight degrees. Brands include Amela from Shizuoka and Nagano and Tokutani from Kochi. Where ordinary tomatoes peak in summer, these are at their sweetest from winter into spring.
