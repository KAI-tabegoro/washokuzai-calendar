How to cook

How to choose

A vivid body colour with well-formed barbels under the chin. Clear eyes.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Okinawa
  • Kagoshima
  • Kochi

Good to know

Two whisker-like barbels under the chin give it its Japanese name, which simply means old man. It is one of the few fish whose formal name is an everyday word. Widely eaten in Okinawa.
