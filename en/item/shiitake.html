How to cook

How to choose

Thick caps curled under at the edge, with clean white gills.

Storage

Do not wash. Store stem-up wrapped in paper or a paper bag in the fridge. Freezing increases the umami.

Main areas

  • Tokushima
  • Hokkaido
  • Iwate
  • Gunma

Good to know

Grown either on logs or on sawdust blocks. Drying increases both vitamin D and umami compounds.
