How to cook

How to choose

A fine, evenly raised netting. Press the base; slight give means it is ready.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature. When the base softens and the aroma strengthens, chill two or three hours before eating.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Kumamoto
  • Hokkaido
  • Yamagata

Good to know

The netting is where the skin split as the fruit grew and then healed. Finer, more even netting is considered better quality.
