How to cook

How to choose

Clear stripes and a crisp sound when tapped, with a slightly sunken stem base.

Storage

Keep whole at room temperature. Once cut, wrap and refrigerate, then eat soon.

Main areas

  • Kumamoto
  • Chiba
  • Yamagata
  • Tottori

Good to know

The centre is the sweetest. Cut in wedges radiating from the middle so everyone gets an equal share of it.
