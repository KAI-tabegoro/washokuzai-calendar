How to cook

How to choose

Blooms not yet fully open, in a vivid yellow, with no wilting or discolouration.

Storage

Refrigerate in the pack. Sensitive to drying, so keep it sealed. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Aichi
  • Shizuoka
  • Fukuoka
  • Nagano

Good to know

A snapdragon grown for the table, named in Japanese for the way the flower resembles a swimming goldfish. It is raised differently from ornamental stock so that it can be eaten. It has almost no flavour; its role is to bring colour and a sense of season to a dish.
