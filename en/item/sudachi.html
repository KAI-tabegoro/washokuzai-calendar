Deep green, glossy skin, heavy in the hand and free of blemishes.

Bag and refrigerate. The juice can also be squeezed and frozen.

Good to know

Almost all Japanese production comes from Tokushima. Kabosu, a related citrus, comes mainly from Oita and is larger.
