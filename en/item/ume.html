How to cook

How to choose

Choose by use: hard green ume for liqueur and syrup, ripe yellow fruit for umeboshi. Avoid any with blemishes.

Storage

Process green ume before it ripens. It cannot be eaten raw and must always be processed.

Main areas

  • Wakayama
  • Gunma
  • Fukui
  • Aomori

Good to know

Nanko ume from Wakayama is famous. Raw ume stones contain harmful compounds, so the fruit must always be processed before eating.
