How to cook

How to choose

Look for clear, domed eyes and bright red gills. The body should feel firm, with well-formed scutes (the row of spiny scales near the tail).

Storage

Best eaten the day you buy it. To keep, remove gills and guts, pat dry, wrap and chill. From the next day on, cook rather than eat raw.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Shimane
  • Ehime
  • Chiba

Good to know

Available year-round, but richest just before spawning in early summer. Fish that stay in one bay rather than migrating are called sezuki-aji; they are yellower and prized.
