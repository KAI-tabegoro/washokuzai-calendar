How to cook

How to choose

Clear banding and a firm body. Clear eyes, with the beak-like teeth intact.

Storage

The firm flesh keeps well. Gut and refrigerate, using within two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Mie
  • Shizuoka
  • Kochi

Good to know

Young fish carry seven clear black bands, which fade with age; old males develop a black snout and are called kuchiguro, black mouth. Its powerful teeth crush the shells of molluscs and sea urchins.
