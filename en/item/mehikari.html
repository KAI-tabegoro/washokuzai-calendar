How to choose

Large, clear blue-green eyes. A firm body with a silver sheen.

Storage

The high fat content spoils quickly. Gut and refrigerate, using the same or the next day.

Main areas

  • Fukushima
  • Miyazaki
  • Kochi
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

Its formal name is aomeeso. Named for its large eyes that glow blue-green, it lives at depths of two to six hundred metres. Once dismissed as a trash fish, its fattiness and ease of eating made it a speciality of Iwaki in Fukushima and Nobeoka in Miyazaki.
