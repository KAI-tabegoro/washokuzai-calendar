How to cook

How to choose

Shucked oysters should be plump and glossy, a creamy white. Avoid shrunken ones.

Storage

Raw-grade and cooking-grade are used differently. Even raw-grade should be eaten within one or two days.

Main areas

  • Hiroshima
  • Miyagi
  • Okayama
  • Hyogo

Good to know

Called the milk of the sea. The difference between raw and cooking grade is not freshness but the hygiene standard of the harvest waters.
