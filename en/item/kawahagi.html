How to cook

How to choose

A large, swollen liver, a firm body and clear eyes.

Storage

Skin, gut and refrigerate. Keep the liver separately and use it quickly.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Ehime
  • Kanagawa
  • Oita

Good to know

The name comes from how easily the skin peels off. In winter the liver fattens, earning it the nickname sea foie gras.
