How to cook

How to choose

A vivid body colour with red gills. A large head and a firm body.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The firm flesh holds for two to three days.

Main areas

  • Nagasaki
  • Hyogo
  • Fukuoka
  • Aichi

Good to know

Known by many regional names, among them gashira, arakabu and bokka. The head is large and there is little to eat, yet the flesh is firm and deeply flavoured. It bears live young rather than laying eggs.
