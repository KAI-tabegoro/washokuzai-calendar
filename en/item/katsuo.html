How to cook

How to choose

In fillets, the bloodline should be vivid and the flesh translucent. It darkens as time passes.

Storage

Discolours quickly, so eat the same day. Wrap fillets tightly against the flesh and refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Shizuoka
  • Miyagi
  • Mie

Good to know

Spring hatsu-gatsuo is lean and clean; autumn modori-gatsuo is rich with fat. The same fish tastes very different.
