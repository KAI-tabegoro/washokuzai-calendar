How to cook

How to choose

Still soil-covered, without cracks, and even in thickness. Very thick roots can be hollow inside.

Storage

Store with the soil on, wrapped in newspaper somewhere cool and dark. Washed roots dry out, so refrigerate them.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Ibaraki
  • Hokkaido
  • Miyazaki

Good to know

Rarely eaten outside Japan. The aroma sits near the skin, so scrape rather than peel.
