How to cook

How to choose

Even thickness with glossy skin, firm in the hand, and a cut end not yet dried.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. Sensitive to cold, so do not over-chill.

Main areas

  • Miyazaki
  • Nagano
  • Chiba
  • Gunma

Good to know

It looks like a cucumber but belongs to the squash family, and it is the immature fruit that is eaten. The flowers are edible too, and in Italy they are stuffed and fried. It only became common in Japan from the 1980s.
