How to cook

How to choose

A pale pink body with clear eyes. A large, squared head and good thickness through the body.

Storage

The flesh is soft and watery. Gut it, salt it lightly and refrigerate to firm it up. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Fukui
  • Kyoto
  • Nagasaki
  • Shimane

Good to know

Three kinds exist: red, white and yellow tilefish. Almost all that reaches the market is the red. The white, called shiro-kuji, is caught in very small numbers and is considered the finest of all. Known as guji in Kyoto, where fish from Wakasa Bay are especially prized. Because they were lightly salted for the journey to Kyoto, the wakasa-yaki method was born.
