How to choose

A thick, deep shell, heavy in the hand and tightly closed.

Storage

Wrap in damp newspaper in the shell and refrigerate. Raw-grade should be eaten the same day.

Main areas

  • Shimane
  • Ishikawa
  • Niigata
  • Akita

Good to know

Where the Pacific oyster is at its best in winter, the rock oyster comes into season in summer, which is unusual. Its spawning is spread over a long period, so it does not thin out in the warm months. It grows two to four years longer, giving a larger shell, more meat and a deeper flavour.
