How to cook

How to choose

A black, glossy body with clear eyes and a firm belly.

Storage

The high fat content oxidises easily. Gut and refrigerate, using within two days.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Shizuoka
  • Tokyo
  • Kanagawa

Good to know

A different fish from nodoguro, the blackthroat seaperch; this one is black on the outside. A deep-water fish, its mouth is black inside as well. So rich in fat that it too is sometimes called the toro of white fish.
