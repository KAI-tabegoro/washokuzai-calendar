How to cook

How to choose

Deep, even skin colour with no cuts or hollows, and heavy in the hand.

Storage

Sensitive to cold. Avoid the fridge; wrap in newspaper and keep somewhere cool and dark at room temperature.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Ibaraki
  • Chiba
  • Miyazaki

Good to know

Stored one to two months after harvest it grows sweeter. Heating slowly at low temperature lets enzymes work and sweetens it further.
