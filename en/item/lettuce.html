How to cook

How to choose

Light in the hand and loosely furled, with a small white cut at the base.

Storage

Press damp paper to the core, bag and refrigerate. Once cut, use soon.

Main areas

  • Nagano
  • Ibaraki
  • Gunma
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

A heavy head means it has grown too far and turned bitter, so choose light ones. Cut surfaces brown because of polyphenols, which tearing by hand keeps in check. Summer crops come from the Nagano highlands, winter ones from Ibaraki and Shizuoka.
