How to cook

How to choose

Heavy in the hand with taut skin. If the leaves are attached, they should look lively.

Storage

Cut the leaves off before storing, or they draw moisture from the root. Wrap in newspaper and refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori
  • Kagoshima

Good to know

The top is sweet and it grows sharper towards the tip. Use the top for salads and the tip for grating.
