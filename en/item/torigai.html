How to choose

A black, glossy foot that is large and plump.

Storage

Loses freshness quickly. Scald it, refrigerate, and use the same or the next day.

Main areas

  • Kyoto
  • Aichi
  • Mie
  • Oita

Good to know

The shape of the foot resembles a bird's beak, which gives it its name. Tango torigai from Kyoto is a farmed luxury brand that grows larger than the wild shellfish.
