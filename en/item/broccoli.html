How to cook

How to choose

Tight, deep green florets, with no hollow at the cut end of the stem.

Storage

Bag it raw and refrigerate standing upright. Boiled, it also freezes.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Saitama
  • Aichi
  • Kagawa

Good to know

What is eaten is a cluster of unopened flower buds; left alone, they open into yellow flowers. In 2026 it was added to Japan's list of designated vegetables, placing it alongside cabbage and daikon as a staple crop. The stem is nutritious too and need not be discarded.
