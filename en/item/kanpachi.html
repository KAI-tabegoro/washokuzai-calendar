How to cook

How to choose

Clear eyes and a firm body. Fillets should have a vivid bloodline and translucent pink flesh.

Storage

Firmer flesh means it keeps better than buri. Pat fillets dry, wrap and refrigerate; use within about two days.

Main areas

  • Kagoshima
  • Kochi
  • Ehime
  • Nagasaki

Good to know

A diagonal dark band above the eye makes the head look like the kanji for eight when seen from the front, which is where the name comes from. It often fetches a higher price than buri.
