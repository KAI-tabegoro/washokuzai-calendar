How to cook

How to choose

Thick, deeply coloured leaves with sturdy stems, not wilted.

Storage

Wrap in damp newspaper and refrigerate standing upright. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Ibaraki
  • Saitama
  • Fukuoka
  • Tokyo

Good to know

Less astringent than spinach, so it can be used without parboiling. The name comes from the Komatsugawa area of Tokyo where it was grown.
