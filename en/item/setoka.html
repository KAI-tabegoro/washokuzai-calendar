How to cook

How to choose

A thin, glossy, deep orange rind and a fruit that feels heavy for its size.

Storage

Bag it against drying and keep it in the vegetable drawer. The thin rind bruises easily, so eat it soon.

Main areas

  • Ehime
  • Saga
  • Nagasaki
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

Bred by crossing Kiyomi with Encore and then crossing the result with Murcott; registered in 2001. It is named after the Hayasaki Strait (seto) near Kuchinotsu in Nagasaki, where it was bred. Its melting flesh has earned it the nickname "the otoro of citrus".
