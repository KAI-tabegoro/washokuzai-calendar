How to cook

How to choose

A vivid body colour with a reddish tinge around the mouth. Good thickness through the body.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The firm flesh holds for two to three days.

Main areas

  • Okinawa
  • Kagoshima
  • Nagasaki
  • Kochi

Good to know

Known as taman in Okinawa, where it is one of the prefecture's prized fish. The pursed mouth looks as though it were whistling, which is where the Japanese name comes from.
