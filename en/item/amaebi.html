How to cook

How to choose

Translucent shells with heads not darkened, and firm flesh.

Storage

Loses freshness quickly. Remove the heads, refrigerate and use the same day. It also freezes.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Niigata
  • Toyama
  • Ishikawa

Good to know

Its formal name is hokkoku-akaebi. It suits raw eating more than cooking because the amino acids behind its sweetness change with heat. Like the botan shrimp it is born male and turns female. It lives at depths of two hundred to six hundred metres.
