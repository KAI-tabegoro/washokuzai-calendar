How to cook

How to choose

White, translucent flesh with some spring to it. Fillets should not be ragged.

Storage

High water content makes it spoil easily. Salting to draw out water before cooking helps.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori
  • Iwate
  • Ishikawa

Good to know

The winter milt is called tachiko or kikuko and is prized. The Japanese word for eating your fill is said to derive from this fish.
