How to cook

How to choose

A hard, angular body with a well-developed dorsal fin, and clear eyes.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Hokkaido
  • Aomori

Good to know

Its formal name is tokubire. The cross-section looks eight-sided, which is why it is called hakkaku, meaning eight corners. Males develop large dorsal and anal fins and fetch higher prices than females.
