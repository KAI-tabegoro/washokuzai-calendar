How to cook

How to choose

Unbroken shells with firm meat that reacts when touched.

Storage

Purge the sand, then refrigerate. Use within one or two days.

Main areas

  • Kumamoto
  • Fukuoka
  • Aichi
  • Chiba

Good to know

Named for its long tubular shell. Pouring salt into its hole on a tidal flat makes it leap out, which has made it a favourite of clam diggers. Overcooking turns it tough, so work quickly.
