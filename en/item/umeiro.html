How to choose

A vivid yellow along the back. A firm body and clear eyes.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Kagoshima
  • Nagasaki
  • Okinawa

Good to know

The yellow running from the back to the tail resembles the colour of a ripe ume plum, which is where the name comes from. Caught on the rocky coasts of southern Japan and treated locally as a luxury fish.
