How to cook

How to choose

Deep green leaves that feel taut and show no wilting, deeply lobed and strongly scented.

Storage

Wrap in damp paper, seal and refrigerate. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Shizuoka
  • Ibaraki
  • Fukuoka
  • Nagano

Good to know

A brassica native to the Mediterranean, known in Japanese as kibana-suzushiro. Its sesame-like scent and gentle bitterness make it essential to Italian cooking. It spread in Japan from the 1990s. Older leaves turn more bitter, so younger ones eat better.
