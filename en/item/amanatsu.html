How to cook

How to choose

A taut rind and a fruit that feels heavy for its size, evenly and deeply coloured.

Storage

Keep somewhere cool, dark and airy. Bag it for the vegetable drawer if it starts to dry.

Main areas

  • Kumamoto
  • Kagoshima
  • Ehime
  • Wakayama

Good to know

Properly called Kawano natsudaidai. It was found in 1935 in Tsukumi, Oita, as a branch sport of the natsumikan. It is gentler in acid than its parent and easier to eat. Picked fruit is too sharp at first, so it is rested for a time to let the acid fall before it ships.
