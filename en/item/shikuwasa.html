How to choose

Green fruit should be deep green, glossy and taut. Ripe fruit turns yellow to orange.

Storage

Bag green fruit and refrigerate. Squeezed juice freezes well.

Main areas

  • Okinawa

Good to know

A small citrus that grows wild in Okinawa and Taiwan. The name is said to come from Okinawan words meaning "to feed sourness". From summer to autumn the green fruit is used for juice; in winter the ripe yellow fruit is eaten fresh. Ogimi village in northern Okinawa Island is the main producer.
