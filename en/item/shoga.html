How to cook

How to choose

Glossy skin, plump and firm. Young ginger should be white with pink at the base.

Storage

Sensitive to drying and cold. Wrap in newspaper and keep somewhere cool and dark. Refrigerate young ginger and use it soon.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Kumamoto
  • Chiba
  • Wakayama

Good to know

Stored mature ginger is pungent and strongly aromatic, suiting condiments and cooked dishes. The young ginger of summer holds more water, is tender and milder, and goes into sweet vinegar pickles. Leaf ginger is eaten raw with miso. Kochi grows more than half the national crop.
