How to cook

How to choose

A silver sheen with bright red gills. Clear eyes and a firm belly.

Storage

Gut and refrigerate. The flesh turns watery easily, so salting it lightly before storage firms it up. Use within two days.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Aichi
  • Hyogo
  • Fukuoka

Good to know

A fish that changes name as it grows: seigo, then fukko, then suzuki. Its season is summer, and in Kyoto it stands alongside hamo as a fish of the hot months.
