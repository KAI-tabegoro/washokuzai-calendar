How to cook

How to choose

Clear black spots along the flank and a blue sheen. The belly should be firm and the eyes not cloudy.

Storage

Spoils quickly, so eat the same day. To keep, remove the head and guts, salt, and refrigerate.

Main areas

  • Chiba
  • Ibaraki
  • Nagasaki
  • Tottori

Good to know

Sardines caught during the rainy season are called nyubai-iwashi and are considered the fattest of the year.
