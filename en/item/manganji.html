How to cook

How to choose

Deep green and glossy, thick-walled and straight, with a fresh cut at the stem.

Storage

Bag and refrigerate. Use within three or four days.

Main areas

  • Kyoto

Good to know

A Kyoto vegetable from the Manganji district of Maizuru, bred in the Taisho era by crossing the Fushimi pepper with a Californian variety. About fifteen centimetres long and thick-walled, it has almost no heat and a sweetness to it, which has earned it the name king of peppers.
