How to cook

How to choose

Deep black and glossy. Held to the light, it should be evenly translucent.

Storage

Sensitive to humidity. Keep sealed with a desiccant and use quickly once opened.

Main areas

  • Saga
  • Hyogo
  • Fukuoka
  • Kumamoto

Good to know

The Ariake Sea in Saga is the largest source. The first winter harvest, ichiban-zumi, is the most tender and fragrant.
