How to cook

How to choose

Steaks should be pale pink and translucent with a vivid bloodline, and free of weeping liquid.

Storage

Pat steaks dry, wrap and refrigerate, using within two days. They also freeze.

Main areas

  • Miyagi
  • Kochi
  • Wakayama
  • Chiba

Good to know

Though called a tuna in Japanese, it belongs to the marlin and swordfish families, not the tuna genus. The extended upper jaw is used to strike at prey. Swordfish is fatty and tender, which makes it the mainstay of steaks, while striped marlin is firmer and prized for sashimi.
