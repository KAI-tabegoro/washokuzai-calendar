How to cook

How to choose

Thick, broad leaves of a deep green, with a firm white base.

Storage

Sensitive to drying. Wrap in damp paper and refrigerate standing upright. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Kochi
  • Tochigi
  • Ibaraki
  • Miyazaki

Good to know

Cut back, it grows again from the crown, and can be harvested several times a year. The first cutting of early spring has tender leaves and a gentler scent, and is the most highly regarded. It has long been treated as a medicinal herb in China.
