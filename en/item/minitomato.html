How to cook

How to choose

Deep, even colour with a green taut calyx, and no splits in the skin.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature, then refrigerate. Over-chilling dulls the taste.

Main areas

  • Kumamoto
  • Aichi
  • Hokkaido
  • Ibaraki

Good to know

Higher in sugar and denser in nutrients than large tomatoes. Varieties such as Aiko and Carol are numerous, and the colours run beyond red to yellow, orange, green and near-black. Fruit shipped on the truss keeps its freshness longer.
