How to cook

How to choose

Evenly orange, with a soft fuzz and a good scent. Fruit with green left on it is sharply sour.

Storage

It does not keep; refrigerate and eat within two or three days. Cook what you cannot finish.

Main areas

  • Aomori
  • Nagano

Good to know

Native to China, it reached Japan in ancient times. Fresh apricots bruise easily and are rarely seen far from where they are grown. The Mori district of Chikuma in Nagano is known as the "village of apricots". The kernel inside the stone gives almond tofu its scent.
