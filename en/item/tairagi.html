How to cook

How to choose

A large, thick adductor muscle with a translucent look.

Storage

Pat the adductor dry, wrap and refrigerate. Use within one or two days.

Main areas

  • Fukuoka
  • Saga
  • Aichi
  • Mie

Good to know

It stands with its large triangular shell driven into the sand. The part eaten is the big adductor muscle. The Ariake Sea was the main source, but poor catches in recent years have pushed prices sharply higher.
