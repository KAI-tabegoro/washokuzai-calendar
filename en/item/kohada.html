How to cook

How to choose

Clear spots along the flank and a bright silver sheen, with firm flesh.

Storage

Loses freshness quickly; it is normally cured in vinegar the same day. Cured, it holds two or three days.

Main areas

  • Saga
  • Kumamoto
  • Aichi
  • Shizuoka

Good to know

A fish that changes name as it grows: shinko, kohada, nakazumi, then konoshiro. The first shinko of summer fetches high prices and is where Edomae sushi chefs test their skill. Its name was said to be shunned by samurai for sounding like the burning of a castle.
