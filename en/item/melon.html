How to cook

How to choose

A fine, evenly raised netting. Press the base; slight give means it is ready.

Storage

Ripen at room temperature. When the base softens and the aroma strengthens, chill two or three hours before eating.

Main areas

  • Shizuoka
  • Ibaraki
  • Kumamoto
  • Aichi

Good to know

A melon with pale green flesh. The Earl's type is the best known, and greenhouse fruit from Shizuoka ships almost all year. It is prized for its scent and its refined sweetness. The netting is scar tissue from the skin splitting as the fruit swells; the finer and more even it is, the better the melon.
