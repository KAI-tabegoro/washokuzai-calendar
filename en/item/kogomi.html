How to cook

How to choose

Tips tightly coiled on short thick stems, deep green and downy.

Storage

Boil, then refrigerate and use within two or three days. It also freezes once boiled.

Main areas

  • Yamagata
  • Akita
  • Niigata
  • Fukushima

Good to know

Its formal name is kusa-sotetsu. The coiled new shoot suggests a person stooping, which gives it its Japanese name. Unusually among mountain vegetables it has almost no bitterness and needs only blanching, which makes it easy to handle. Slipperiness and bite are its virtues.
