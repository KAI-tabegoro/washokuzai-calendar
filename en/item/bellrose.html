How to cook

How to choose

Buds firm and tightly closed, in a vivid red, with undamaged petal edges.

Storage

Refrigerate in the pack. Sensitive to drying and heat. Use within two or three days.

Main areas

  • Aichi

Good to know

A brand of edible miniature rose grown under glass in Toyohashi, Aichi. It is raised with minimal chemicals so that it can be eaten, shipped in bud and packed one bloom to a cell. Edible, though its main role is to bring elegance to a dish.
